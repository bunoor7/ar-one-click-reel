## Word meaning → paper motion

### Length, extension
**Cue words:** طويل، يمتد، بعيد، سنين، مسافة

**Idea:** A paper strip or accordion fold extends across the frame with the word

1. Assembly beat: an accordion paper strip unfolds piece by piece, or a paper ribbon unrolls
2. Code layer: 13-line-draw or 15-marker-draw under the still title

### Shortness, shrinking
**Cue words:** قصير، صغير، يتقلص، قليل

**Idea:** A piece folds in on itself

1. Assembly beat: the paper folds closed into a small square

### Weight, importance
**Cue words:** ثقيل، أهم، خطير، ضخم

**Idea:** The main piece drops last and settles with a heavy stepped bounce and a deep contact shadow

1. Assembly beat: drop and settle on twos

### Fall, collapse
**Cue words:** سقط، انهار، خسارة، تراجع

**Idea:** A paper piece tips and falls, layers slump

1. Assembly beat: a cut-out topples off its stand
2. Code layer: 28-3d-bars collapsing, data from Stage 3

### Rise, growth
**Cue words:** ارتفع، صعد، نما، زاد

**Idea:** A pop-up lifts on its paper tab, a paper plant unfolds upward

1. Assembly beat: pop-up lift
2. Code layer: 40-count-up

### Joining, unity
**Cue words:** اجتمع، اتحد، معًا، يكمّل

**Idea:** Torn pieces slide together into one shape

1. Assembly beat: torn halves meet and the seam closes
2. Code layer: 06-paper-collage

### Splitting, division
**Cue words:** انقسم، تفرّق، خلاف، تفكك

**Idea:** A sheet tears in two and the halves drift apart

1. Assembly beat: a visible paper tear

### Shock, surprise
**Cue words:** صدمة، فجأة، ضربة

**Idea:** A stamp slams onto paper, or a sheet crumples

1. Assembly beat: stamp punch with paper dust
2. Generated clip: strong beat about every 3 s (video-generator-tips §2)

### Speed
**Cue words:** سريع، فورًا، بلحظة

**Idea:** Pieces snap in fast, fewer poses

1. Assembly beat: quick snaps on ones for that moment only

### Slowness, waiting
**Cue words:** ببطء، انتظر، صبر، هدوء

**Idea:** A long living-paper hold, tiny motion only

1. Living-paper prompt: prompt-templates §8

### Reveal, secret
**Cue words:** كشف، الحقيقة، سر، مخفي

**Idea:** A paper flap lifts or a fold opens to show what is under it

1. Assembly beat: flap lift

### Before and after
**Cue words:** قبل، بعد، كان، صار

**Idea:** A page turns from one state to the other

1. Assembly beat: page turn
2. Code layer: 20-before-after

### Many, spread
**Cue words:** كثير، الكل، العالم، انتشر

**Idea:** Cut-outs multiply in rows

1. Assembly beat: rows of identical cut-outs fill in
2. Code layer: 03-globe-arcs

### Path, steps
**Cue words:** طريق، مسار، خطوة، ثم

**Idea:** A paper string or a dotted route draws across a paper map

1. Code layer: 42-flow-line
2. Assembly beat: stepping-stone pieces placed one by one

**Cue words:** داخل، طبقات، نظام، تحت

**Idea:** Layers separate in depth with parallax

## Rules

- Arabic text never moves: titles are still layers added in the edit (arabic_title.py). Never letter-by-letter. Animations 01, 07, 10 and 33 move single letters and are not used on Arabic.
- Paper stays paper: no morphing, melting or CGI-smooth motion; stop-motion on twos; real thickness and contact shadows.
- The camera is locked by default; it moves only in the continuous-camera option.
- Code layers: two or three per film at most, one per shot; colours and fonts from the film.
- Quoted verses and hadith never move; no music under them; nothing playful on serious or religious topics.
- Never show a prophet or companion in any form (content-and-integrity.md).
- Vary: never the same motion family on two consecutive scenes; keep one hero motion for the strongest moment.
